## Transformations and controls

- HumanEval+ contributes 164 tasks and MBPP+ 399 tasks; all 563 available tasks are used in Phase 1 and Phase 6.
- Initial generation has one stochastic response per model/task at temperature 1.0 and no fixed backend seed.
- Detection and tool-assisted phases use temperature 0.0; thinking/reasoning effort is disabled where supported.
- Train/validation/test allocation is deterministic with seed 42 and grouped by task. Validation uses 15% and test 20% of task groups; the held-out evaluation has 113 tasks and 3,729 rationale-expanded rows.
- Duplicate evaluation attempts caused by retries are resolved by the persisted key `(model_id, provider kind, sample_index)`, retaining the last row. Infrastructure-error rows are excluded from metric denominators and reported as coverage.
- Multilabel metrics are computed without primary-label reduction. Primary-label reduction is used only for the confusion-matrix visualization and is declared in its caption.
- Phase-6 and Phase-7 results are isolated from the Phase-1/Phase-2 training data.

## Known unavailable artifacts on this machine

The external-SSD raw files for the eleven local SLM conditions in Phases 6 and 7 are referenced by configuration but are not mounted. Their absence blocks regeneration of inferential statistics for those rows; existing manuscript point estimates are retained as descriptive results pending restoration of the SSD. No values are reconstructed from the manuscript.
